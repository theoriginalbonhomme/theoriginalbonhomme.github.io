A good man, on the road

In the Cathar lands of southern France, people called the most devoted believers bonshommes: good men. BONHOMME carries that name into dance music culture: music made by loving humans, in service of art.

Genre doesn’t matter. What matters is that the music is good and the sound is perfect.

No home base. Twenty years travelling the world, on the road and at sea. Every place leaves its sound in the music.
